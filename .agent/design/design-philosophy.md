## 📐 Section-by-Section Design Philosophy

### 1. Hero / Introduction
> **Goal:** 3-second wow factor. Instant recognition and human connection.
- **Freestanding 3D Cutout**: The developer's portrait is a freestanding cutout reacting dynamically to cursor position (`Matrix4` 3D perspective tilt).
- **Inverted Kinetic Typography**: Massive **FAZAL E-HAQ** lettering floats behind the portrait in faint charcoal ink, shifting in the opposite direction of cursor motion for tangible spatial depth.
- **Hand-Crafted CTAs**: Outlined sketch buttons for exploring works and downloading the verified resume.

### 2. About
> **Goal:** Show depth of journey, technical versatility, and personal story.
- **Bento Grid Architecture**: Asymmetric arrangement of sketch cards.
- **The Journey**: Narrative card with career milestones and real-time "Available for work" glowing indicator.
- **Interactive Tech Stack**: Hand-drawn chips categorized by Languages, Frameworks, Backend/DB, and Design tools.
- **Services & Skills**: Expressive cards highlighting mobile development, UI/UX prototyping, and product design.

### 3. Featured Works
> **Goal:** Proof of engineering and design craftsmanship.
- **Horizontal 3D Carousel**: Hand-drawn showcase cards allowing smooth horizontal scrolling across projects.
- **Rich Project Detail**: Logos, screenshots, tag badges (`Flutter`, `Dart`, `Isar DB`, `Provider`), and direct repository links.
- **Zero Heavy Overlays**: Pure outlines and readable contrast on sketchbook paper.

### 4. Contact
> **Goal:** Lowest friction path to collaborate.
- **Clean Sketch Form**: Centered sketch container with hand-drawn input fields and focus indicators.
- **Instant Actions**: Direct email compose and social badges (GitHub, LinkedIn, Instagram).
- **Friendly Voice**: Welcoming, warm tone encouraging dialogue.

### 5. Floating Navigation & Spatial HUD
> **Goal:** Effortless orientation without clutter.
- **Desktop Floating Pill**: Center-aligned outline pill header with subtle wobble and instant waypoint jumping.
- **Mobile Bottom Bar**: Compact bottom navigation pill for ergonomic thumb reach.
- **Spatial HUD Spine**: Right-side vertical progression track displaying live scroll percentage (`0% → 100%`) and active section dots.
