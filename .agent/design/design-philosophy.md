## Section Design Philosophy

### 1. Hero / Introduction
> **Goal:** Instant identity. 3-second understanding.
- Full-viewport cinematic entrance
- Name in massive Unbounded typography
- Animated role/title (typewriter, scramble, or morph)
- Subtle atmospheric background (gradient mesh, particles, or geometric)
- Primary CTA: "View My Work" / Secondary: "Download Resume"
- Decorative code snippets or UI elements floating in background

### 2. About
> **Goal:** Human connection. "I'd want to work with this person."
- Professional photo (optional, with treatment)
- Concise bio — personality + skills, not a resume dump
- Tech stack visualization (interactive, not a boring icon grid)
- Journey timeline or career highlights
- Services offered (if applicable)

### 3. Projects / Work
> **Goal:** Proof of excellence. "This person ships quality."
- Featured projects (2-4 max) with rich, immersive cards
- Each project: screenshot/video, title, description, tech stack, links
- Hover/click reveals more detail
- Visual variety — not just a grid of identical cards
- Filter/category (if many projects, otherwise curated selection)

### 4. Contact
> **Goal:** Lowest friction path to hire/connect.
- Clear headline: "Let's Build Something Together"
- Direct email link (one click to compose)
- Social links (GitHub, LinkedIn, Twitter/X)
- Optional: minimal contact form
- Warm, inviting tone — not corporate

### 5. Navigation
> **Goal:** Effortless orientation. Always know where you are.
- Fixed top nav on desktop (glassmorphic on scroll)
- Bottom nav on mobile
- Active section indicator (animated underline or dot)
- Smooth scroll to section on click
- Logo/name always visible as home anchor

## Visual Techniques

### Glassmorphism
- Semi-transparent backgrounds with backdrop blur
- Subtle borders (white/accent at low opacity)
- Used for: nav bar, cards, modals, overlays
- NOT overused — max 2-3 glass elements visible at once

### Gradient Mesh / Atmospheric Background
- Subtle, large-scale gradient blobs in the background
- Colors: accent at very low opacity (5-15%)
- Adds depth without competing with content
- Animate slowly for living, breathing feel

### Text as Design Element
- Oversized background text (section labels at massive scale, very low opacity)
- Typography as the hero element — Unbounded at 72px+ makes a statement
- Monospace accents for code-inspired touches

### Micro-Interactions
- Cursor effects (subtle trail, magnetic buttons)
- Text scramble/decode effects on hover
- Card tilt on mouse position (parallax)
- Smooth number counting for statistics
- Custom scrollbar styling

## What NOT to Do

- ❌ No stock illustrations or generic SVG art
- ❌ No gradient overload (max 1 prominent gradient per viewport)
- ❌ No parallax scrolling that causes motion sickness
- ❌ No auto-playing sounds or videos
- ❌ No popups, modals on entry, or newsletter signups
- ❌ No "under construction" or "coming soon" sections
- ❌ No generic template feel — this must feel handcrafted
- ❌ No loading screens longer than 2 seconds
